Pest Problems We Solve Instantly

Each pest carries health risks and damages property. We target the source, not just the surface.

🪳

Cockroaches

Health risk: Carry Salmonella, E. coli and trigger asthma attacks. They multiply rapidly — one female can produce up to 400 offspring in her lifetime.

Damage: Contaminate food, utensils and surfaces with bacteria-laden droppings.
🐀

Rats & Mice

Health risk: Spread leptospirosis, hantavirus and salmonellosis through urine and droppings. Their bites can also transmit infections.

Damage: Chew through electrical wires (fire hazard), insulation, furniture and even structural wood. They gnaw constantly to wear down their teeth.
🛏️

Bedbugs

Health risk: Painful, itchy bites that can become infected from scratching. They feed on human blood while you sleep, causing anxiety and insomnia.

Damage: Not known to transmit diseases, but their presence causes significant psychological distress. They can hide in mattresses, furniture crevices and behind wallpapers.
🐜

Termites

Health risk: No direct health risks to humans, but they can cause allergic reactions and asthma from airborne particles.

Damage: Costly structural damage — they consume wood from the inside out, weakening floors, walls and foundations. They cause over KES 500 million in property damage annually in Kenya.
🦟

Mosquitoes

Health risk: Major carriers of malaria, dengue fever, chikungunya and Zika virus. In Kenya, malaria remains a leading cause of illness and death.

Damage: They breed in standing water — a single mosquito can lay 100–300 eggs at a time. We treat both adult mosquitoes and their breeding sites.
🐜

Ants & Others

Health risk: Ants can contaminate food with bacteria from their foraging trails. Some species like fire ants deliver painful stings.

Damage: Carpenter ants tunnel through wood, causing structural damage similar to termites but at a slower rate. Sugar ants and other species invade kitchens and pantries.
