For crawling insects like cockroaches and ants, you'll see a noticeable reduction within 24 hours. For bedbugs and termites, complete elimination may take 48 hours to 2 weeks depending on the life cycle. We always follow up to confirm. Mosquito fogging shows immediate results, and we recommend staying indoors for 30 minutes after fogging.

Our team will advise you based on your specific service. We'll also let you know if you need to cover food, utensils, or remove plants and aquariums before treatment.

After treatment, we provide a detailed prevention guide that includes: sealing cracks and gaps around windows and doors, storing food in airtight containers, fixing leaky pipes and faucets, keeping your home clean and clutter-free, trimming vegetation away from your house, and installing mesh screens on windows.
