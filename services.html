Our Solution: We apply a safe gel and spray treatment targeting hiding spots, cracks, and drains.

Duration: Usually 1–2 hours depending on house size.

Safety: Safe for children and pets once dry.

Guarantee: 14‑day free retreatment if pests return.

The Problem: Rats damage property and pose health risks. They breed quickly and are hard to eliminate.

Our Solution: We use a combination of baits, traps, and proofing to seal entry points and remove rodents permanently.

Duration: 2–3 hours depending on property size.

Safety: Baits are placed in tamper‑proof stations, safe for pets and children.
