Professional Pest Control Services

Targeted solutions for every pest problem. Expert technicians, safe products, and a 14-day guarantee.

🪳 Cockroach Control

The Problem: Cockroaches carry Salmonella, E. coli, and trigger asthma. They breed rapidly in warm, dark spaces like kitchens and bathrooms.

Our Solution: We apply advanced gel baits (not just sprays) that target the colony's nest. The bait is ingested by roaches and carried back to the nest, eliminating the entire population. We also treat cracks, crevices, and drains with residual sprays.

Duration: 1-2 hours.

Preparation: Clear kitchen countertops and floors. Store food in sealed containers. We will do the rest.

Price from: KSh 3,500 (2-bedroom home).

🐀 Rats & Mice Control

The Problem: Rats chew through electrical wires (fire hazards), contaminate food, and carry Hantavirus. They breed quickly and are hard to catch with DIY traps.

Our Solution: We use a 3-step approach: (1) Bait stations with tamper-proof blocks, (2) Snap traps for immediate kill, (3) Exclusion services to seal entry points with steel wool and caulking. We don't just kill them; we make sure they can't return.

Duration: 2-3 hours (including proofing).

Preparation: Declutter garages and storage areas. Keep pet food sealed.

Price from: KSh 3,500 (2-bedroom home).

🛏️ Bedbug Treatment

The Problem: Bedbugs cause itchy, red bites and severe anxiety. They hide in mattress seams, behind headboards, and inside furniture joints. They are immune to many over-the-counter sprays.

Our Solution: We combine high-heat steam treatment (to instantly kill eggs and adults) with residual insecticide sprays. This dual-action ensures all life stages are eradicated, not just the adults.

Duration: 1-2 hours per room.

Preparation: Wash all bedding and clothes in hot water and dry on high heat before our visit.

Price from: KSh 4,500 (per bedroom).

🐜 Termite Prevention & Treatment

The Problem: Termites eat wood from the inside out, causing structural collapses. They often go unnoticed until major damage is done.

Our Solution: We apply a liquid termiticide barrier around your foundation and install Sentricon-style bait stations in the soil. Workers carry the bait back to the queen, poisoning the entire colony within 60 days.

Duration: 1 day for treatment; 3-month monitoring cycle.

Price from: KSh 6,000 (2-bedroom home).

Book Termite Treatment

🦟 Mosquito Control

The Problem: Mosquitoes transmit malaria and dengue. They breed in standing water, even in small puddles or old tires.

Our Solution: We treat all breeding sites (gutters, pots, drains) with larvicides. For adult control, we use safe thermal fogging that reduces mosquito populations by 80% in one session.

Duration: 1-2 hours.

Preparation: Remove any standing water containers before our visit.

Price from: KSh 4,000 (2-bedroom home).

Book Mosquito Control

🐜 Ants & Other Pests

The Problem: Ants invade pantries and contaminate food. Some species (like carpenter ants) can damage wood like termites.

Our Solution: We identify the species (sugar ants, fire ants, carpenter ants) and apply specific gel baits. The ants take the bait back to the queen, killing the colony in days.

Duration: 1 hour.

Price from: KSh 2,500 (2-bedroom home).
